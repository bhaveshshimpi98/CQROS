# CQROS Glossary

**Version:** 1.0.0

**Status:** Draft

**Owner:** CQROS Architecture Team

**Classification:** Master Reference Document

---

# 1. Purpose

This glossary defines the official terminology used throughout the CQROS project.

Its objectives are to:

- Establish a common language.
- Eliminate ambiguity.
- Standardize documentation.
- Ensure consistent communication.
- Provide precise definitions for technical concepts.

Every project document should use these definitions.

---

# 2. General Terms

## CQROS

Cryptocurrency Quantitative Research Operating System.

The complete institutional-grade platform responsible for research, portfolio construction, execution, governance, monitoring, and reporting.

---

## Architecture

The structural organization of CQROS including layers, packages, interfaces, dependencies, and communication rules.

---

## Layer

A logical architectural boundary with one primary responsibility.

Layers communicate only through defined interfaces.

---

## Package

A Python package representing one business domain.

Examples:

- exchange
- storage
- validation
- features
- portfolio

---

## Module

A Python source file implementing a focused responsibility within a package.

---

## Component

A reusable software unit such as a service, repository, validator, registry, or adapter.

---

## Service

A class responsible for executing business logic.

Services coordinate work but do not own infrastructure.

---

## Adapter

A component translating between CQROS and external systems.

Examples:

- BinanceAdapter
- BybitAdapter
- CoinbaseAdapter

Adapters isolate vendor-specific behavior.

---

## Interface

A contract defining expected behavior without implementation.

Interfaces provide loose coupling between components.

---

## Dependency Injection

The process of supplying required dependencies externally instead of creating them internally.

---

# 3. Data Terms

## Dataset

A structured collection of validated market information.

Datasets are immutable once published.

---

## Raw Dataset

Data exactly as received from external providers.

No transformations are applied except normalization required for ingestion.

---

## Research Dataset

A cleaned, validated, synchronized dataset suitable for feature engineering and research.

---

## Dataset Version

A unique identifier representing one immutable snapshot of a dataset.

---

## Dataset Hash

A cryptographic fingerprint verifying dataset integrity.

---

## Metadata

Descriptive information about an artifact.

Metadata may include:

- Version
- Timestamp
- Schema
- Owner
- Lineage
- Hash
- Configuration

---

## Lineage

The complete history describing how an artifact was created.

---

## Artifact

Any versioned output generated by CQROS.

Examples:

- Dataset
- Feature Set
- Target Set
- Model
- Report
- Strategy
- Experiment

---

# 4. Research Terms

## Feature

A deterministic transformation of historical information used as model input.

Examples:

- RSI
- ATR
- Order Book Imbalance
- Funding Rate

---

## Feature Set

A collection of features used together for model training.

---

## Feature Registry

The catalog of all approved engineered features.

---

## Target

The value a model attempts to predict.

Examples:

- 1-hour return
- 24-hour volatility
- Direction label

---

## Target Horizon

The future period represented by a target.

Examples:

- 5 minutes
- 1 hour
- 1 day

---

## Leakage

Use of future information during research.

Leakage invalidates experimental results.

CQROS has zero tolerance for leakage.

---

## Walk-Forward Validation

Evaluation method using sequential training and testing windows.

Preferred for time-series research.

---

## Cross Validation

Repeated model evaluation using multiple training/testing splits.

For time series, only time-aware methods are allowed.

---

## Regime

A market condition classification.

Examples:

- Bull
- Bear
- High Volatility
- Low Volatility
- Sideways

---

## Alpha

Expected excess return generated by a predictive model or strategy.

---

## Signal

A numerical recommendation produced by the Alpha Engine.

Signals are transformed into portfolio allocations.

---

# 5. Statistical Terms

## Information Coefficient (IC)

Correlation between predictions and future outcomes.

Measures predictive quality.

---

## Rank IC

Rank correlation between predictions and future returns.

---

## Sharpe Ratio

Risk-adjusted return metric.

---

## Sortino Ratio

Downside-risk-adjusted return metric.

---

## Maximum Drawdown

Largest peak-to-trough decline.

---

## Volatility

Magnitude of price fluctuations.

---

## Turnover

Frequency with which portfolio positions change.

---

## Capacity

Estimated capital deployable before market impact significantly degrades strategy performance.

---

## Slippage

Difference between expected execution price and actual execution price.

---

## Transaction Cost

Cost incurred while executing trades.

Includes:

- Fees
- Spread
- Slippage

---

# 6. Machine Learning Terms

## Model

A trained predictive algorithm.

---

## Training

The process of fitting a model to historical data.

---

## Inference

Using a trained model to generate predictions.

---

## Hyperparameter

A configurable parameter controlling model behavior.

---

## Feature Importance

Measurement describing each feature's contribution to predictions.

---

## Drift

Change in statistical properties over time.

Types include:

- Feature Drift
- Data Drift
- Concept Drift
- Prediction Drift

---

## Calibration

Agreement between predicted probabilities and observed outcomes.

---

# 7. Portfolio Terms

## Portfolio

Collection of positions managed together.

---

## Allocation

Distribution of capital across assets.

---

## Position

Exposure held in a financial instrument.

---

## Position Size

Capital allocated to one position.

---

## Exposure

Risk associated with holdings.

Examples:

- Long Exposure
- Short Exposure
- Net Exposure
- Gross Exposure

---

## Leverage

Ratio between market exposure and available capital.

---

## Risk Budget

Maximum acceptable contribution of each position to total portfolio risk.

---

# 8. Execution Terms

## Order

Instruction submitted to an exchange.

---

## Fill

Completed execution of an order.

---

## Partial Fill

Execution of only part of an order.

---

## Execution Plan

Instructions describing how a portfolio should be converted into executable orders.

---

## Execution Engine

Subsystem responsible for submitting and monitoring orders.

---

## Paper Trading

Realtime simulated execution without risking capital.

---

## Live Trading

Execution using real capital.

---

# 9. Governance Terms

## Experiment

One complete research run with recorded inputs and outputs.

---

## Experiment Registry

Catalog of all research experiments.

---

## Approval

Formal authorization allowing an artifact to progress to the next lifecycle stage.

---

## Promotion

Moving an approved artifact into production.

---

## Rollback

Returning to a previously approved version.

---

## Policy

A formal rule governing platform behavior.

Policies are configuration-driven whenever possible.

---

## Audit Trail

Chronological history of important system events.

---

# 10. Operational Terms

## Monitoring

Continuous observation of platform health.

---

## Alert

Notification generated when predefined conditions occur.

---

## Health Check

Automated verification that a subsystem is functioning correctly.

---

## Observability

Ability to understand system behavior through logs, metrics, traces, and metadata.

---

## Deployment

Release of a new software version into an environment.

---

## Environment

An isolated execution context.

Examples:

- Development
- Testing
- Staging
- Production

---

# 11. Engineering Terms

## Deterministic

Produces identical outputs when given identical inputs.

---

## Immutable

Cannot be modified after creation.

New versions are created instead.

---

## Reproducibility

Ability to recreate identical research results using recorded artifacts.

---

## Configuration

External definition of system behavior.

Business rules belong in configuration rather than source code.

---

## Registry

A managed collection of versioned artifacts.

---

## Contract

A formal agreement describing inputs, outputs, behavior, and guarantees.

---

## Domain

A distinct business capability represented by one package.

---

## Version

A unique identifier representing a specific immutable state of an artifact.

# 13. Glossary Governance

The glossary is a controlled document.

New terms require:

1. Definition
2. Business context
3. Review
4. Approval
5. Documentation update

All project documentation, code comments, architecture documents, specifications, and AI prompts should reference the terminology defined in this glossary.

---

# 14. Summary

The CQROS Glossary establishes the official vocabulary for the platform.

Using consistent terminology reduces ambiguity, improves communication, simplifies onboarding, and ensures that documentation, implementation, testing, and governance all describe the system in the same way.

This glossary is the authoritative reference for project terminology and evolves alongside the architecture.
